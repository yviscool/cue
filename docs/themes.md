## 3. Physical Keycap Styles

Cue decouples color themes from physical geometry, letting you mix and match any color palette with any keycap style:

- **Liquid Glass (`liquid-glass`)**: 12px rounded corners, translucent backdrop, soft diffusion shadow, and delicate 1px light specular border.
- **Laptop Scissor (`laptop`)**: Ultra-compact 6px radius, shallow travel depth, sleek modern notebook feel.
- **PBT Mechanical (`pbt-mechanical`)**: Sculpted key profile with simulated top-face bevel and bottom stem drop shadow.
- **Minimalist Chalk (`minimal`)**: Borderless, distraction-free typographic layout.
- **Cyber Neon (`cyber-neon`)**: Outer electric glow aura with high-contrast inner phosphor border.
- **Retro Typewriter (`retro-typewriter`)**: Circular or vintage rounded square keycaps with mechanical chrome rim.
- **Fine Outline (`outline`)**: Ultra-crisp 1.5px hairline contour, optimal for minimalist screencasts.
